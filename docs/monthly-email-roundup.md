# Monthly email roundup

## Review and approve

The owner signs in at `/admin/email-roundups`. The page shows the email subject, preview text, complete email, selected changelog items and eligible recipient count. Preparation never sends an email.

On the first of each month at 09:00 Europe/London, `prepareMonthlyRoundup` prepares the previous calendar month's draft. It reads `/email-releases.json` from the deployed website, not from the functions bundle or local working tree. The website build publishes that feed from `src/constants/changelog.ts`.

Drafts suggest four updates, preferring highlights. Choose up to six and update the preview. `roundup: false` excludes a changelog item (use for expired offers or withdrawn features); `roundup: true` allows a fix that would otherwise be excluded. Review dated copy, availability and screenshots before approval. Copy is taken from the changelog, not generated by an AI model.

Refresh rebuilds the content and audience and clears the old review revision. It restores the suggested feature selection. Approval requires the exact reviewed revision and recipient count, and the audience must be less than 24 hours old. The two-step approval queues a real campaign; do not click it during testing.

## Audience and delivery

The snapshot includes verified, enabled Firebase Auth accounts with a corresponding user record, excluding opt-outs, throttled signups, marked test accounts, owner addresses and duplicate email addresses. Free and Pro users are both eligible. Provider suppression still applies; the count is an eligible target count, not a delivery guarantee.

The worker rechecks each account's eligibility and email address immediately before sending. New accounts are not added to an approved snapshot. Each email gets the existing signed unsubscribe link and a stable month/user idempotency key. Content HTML and text are frozen at approval; a later deploy does not regenerate an approved email.

`sendApprovedRoundups` runs every minute, processing at most 100 pending recipients per invocation with a transaction lease and bounded runtime. It only processes approved revisions. Provider acceptance, skips and failures are counted separately. Failed recipients are not automatically retried. Jobs older than 23 hours pause as `needs-review` to avoid replaying uncertain sends beyond the provider idempotency window. Investigate recipient failure reasons and acceptance receipts before considering any manual recovery.

Records are private under `monthlyEmailRoundups/{month}` and its audience subcollections. Access uses the existing verified-owner callable authorization; clients have no direct Firestore permission. Refreshes preserve older audience snapshots for investigation.

## Deployment

Deploy the website first so the public release feed exists, then deploy `manageMonthlyRoundup`, `prepareMonthlyRoundup` and `sendApprovedRoundups`. No new provider integration or keys are required. Existing Firebase and Resend configuration is used.

After deployment, prepare a completed month through the admin page and verify the actual email and recipient count without approving it. The scheduled job does not overwrite an existing monthly record. If preparation fails, the review page shows the error and supports manual refresh.

No customer-facing changelog entry is needed for the admin preparation workflow itself. Nothing is sent or scheduled for recipients until explicit campaign approval.
